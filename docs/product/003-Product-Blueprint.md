# OQOOD Product Blueprint

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

---

# Executive Summary

OQOOD is an AI-powered enterprise Source-to-Contract platform that enables organizations to manage procurement, suppliers, contracts, and project execution through a unified cloud platform.

The platform is designed primarily for construction, industrial, energy, infrastructure, and enterprise organizations operating across the Middle East.

---

# Vision

To become the leading AI-powered Source-to-Contract platform in the Middle East.

---

# Mission

Digitize and automate the complete procurement and contracting lifecycle through secure, intelligent, enterprise-grade software.

---

# Product Goals

• Simplify procurement operations.

• Improve supplier collaboration.

• Reduce procurement cycle time.

• Increase transparency.

• Improve decision making using AI.

• Provide complete auditability.
