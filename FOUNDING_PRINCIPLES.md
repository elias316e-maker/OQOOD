# OQOOD Founding Principles

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

These principles define how OQOOD is designed, developed, and evolved. Every architectural, product, and engineering decision should align with these principles.

---

# 1. Customer Value First

Every feature must solve a real customer problem and deliver measurable business value.

---

# 2. Enterprise Quality by Default

All features should be designed to support enterprise customers without requiring major redesign.

---

# 3. Arabic First, Global Ready

Arabic is the primary language of the platform while maintaining full multilingual capabilities.

---

# 4. AI Is Built-In

Artificial Intelligence is part of the platform architecture, not an optional add-on.

---

# 5. Security by Design

Security, privacy, and compliance are mandatory design requirements.

---

# 6. API First

Every core business capability must be accessible through a well-designed API.

---

# 7. Modular Architecture

The platform is built as independent modules with clear responsibilities and minimal coupling.

---

# 8. Workflow Driven

Business processes are implemented as configurable workflows rather than hardcoded sequences.

---

# 9. Audit Everything

Every significant business action must be traceable through a complete audit trail.

---

# 10. Documentation First

No major feature is implemented before its architecture, requirements, and business rules are documented.

---

# 11. Configuration Over Customization

Business behavior should be controlled through configuration whenever possible instead of custom code.

---

# 12. Cloud Native

The platform is designed for cloud deployment, scalability, resilience, and continuous delivery.

---

# 13. Performance Matters

Performance is a functional requirement. The platform should remain responsive as data and users grow.

---

# 14. Continuous Improvement

Every release should improve usability, maintainability, performance, or business value.

---

# Final Principle

We build OQOOD for the next ten years, not just for the next release.
